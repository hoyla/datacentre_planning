Before you publish

  • The biggest number in a document is almost never the site’s. All twenty-two of the largest MW figures in the corpus describe a national target, a rival or a forecast.
  • Not everything in megawatts is power. Annual energy, thermal input and battery ratings are separated here; they will not be in a raw extract.
  • Per building, per hall or per phase. Where the documents are ambiguous, this release flags rather than multiplies.
