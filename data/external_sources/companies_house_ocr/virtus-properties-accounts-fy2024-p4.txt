VIRTUS Data Centres Properties Limited

Strategic report

for the year ended 31 December 2024

The directors present their Strategic report on VIRTUS Data Centres Properties Limited ("the Company") and its
subsidiaries (the "Group" or "VIRTUS") for the year ended 31 December 2024.

Principal activities

VIRTUS designs, builds, operates and maintains carrier neutral data centres in the United Kingdom and the Group’s
principal business is the provision of reliable, resilient and responsible digital infrastructure to enable delivery of colocation
services within these facilities to our customers.

Strategy

VIRTUS has a guiding vision to be a trusted global leader through being different by design. We aim to be the data centre
provider that customers want to come to, a business that customers want to work with and a company that people want to
work for, and seek to achieve this by delivering outstanding safety, sustainability and customer propositions every day
through our expanding portfolio of data centre sites in the UK.

Business review

During 2024 UK sales have continued to grow across the core sectors of cloud services, education and enterprise,
consistent with the ongoing and increasing demand in all segments of modern society for digital services and their
supporting infrastructure. No new sites went live during 2024 but an additional 6. 3MW at our LONDON7 site and
3.4MW at LONDON11 started billing during the year.

At the end of 2024, VIRTUS had a portfolio of 11 live sites that are 98% contracted to customers (2023: 98%), including a
significant proportion occupied by major hyperscale customers, Our two development sites that are nearing completion -
LONDON(12 at Slough and LONDON 14 at Stockley Park - add a further 43.0MW of sellable capacity, with discussions with
major potential customers for those sites well advanced as at the end of the year.

In the UK, 181.8MW of the total design capacity was contracted to customers, with 179.8MW billable to customers as of
the year-end (2023: 180.7MW contracted and 170.5MW billable).

Group revenue in 2024 was £486.2m (2023: £359.0m) representing an increase of 35% on 2023. This was driven primarily
by growth in power revenue, up 81% to £258.5m (2023: £142.5m) due to significantly higher power prices in the first three
quarters of 2024 compared to the prior year which are passed through to customers, plus higher power draw from both
new and existing customer deployments. These higher input energy costs reduced subsequently from the start of Q4 2024
in line with the wider energy market and hence power input costs and billings to customers have been iower despite the
actual volumes of power being drawn remaining stable. Underlying colocation revenue also grew by 9% to £209.1m
(2023: £192.7m) driven by a 8.7MW increase in billable capacity across existing sites during the year, in particular at
LONDON7 and LONDON11.

The business made an operating profit of £45.2m in 2024, compared to £50.8m in 2023, the decrease being due primarily
to a higher goodwill impairment charge in 2024. The Group's result for the financial year was a net profit of £9.8m (2023:
net loss of £2.9m).

During 2024, the Group funded its ongoing site development and operating activities through drawdowns against debt
facilities and utilisation of cash flow generated from operations.

The Group made a scheduled debt repayment on its main facility of £10.0m on 31 December 2024 (2023: £7.5m), reducing
the remaining facility size to £1,135.3m. Of this, £941.3m was drawn and £194.0m remained available to draw at the end
of 2024, with a total of £231.1m drawn down during the year to fund ongoing site development.

The balance of cash on hand at 31 December 2024 was £35.9m (2023: £32.0m).
