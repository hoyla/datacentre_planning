FOR THE YEAR ENDED 30 JUNE 2025

The directors present their Strategic Report ior the period ended 30 June 2025.

Business review

DataVita delivered strong operational and financial performance during the period, supported by sustained growth in its customer base,
which translated into increased tumover and operating profit. Investment in the company’s Tier Ill data centre remained significant,
while its secondary city centre facility also demonstrated encouraging growth.

During the year, DataVita—working in collaboration with key stakeholders—submitted an Expression of Interest to participate in the UK
Government's initiative to establish Artificial Intelligence (Al) Growth Zones. Following this process, the company's existing Tier Ill data
centre and surrounding development land were formally designated by the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT)
as part of the UK Al Growth Zone programme, creating Scotland's first Al Growth Zone. The Al Growth Zone will enable up to 500 MW
of Al ready data centre capacity, with the potential to attract approximately £8.2 billion of private investment into North Lanarkshire and
the wider region. As part of this initiative, the first phase of build oul represents @ capital commitment of £112 million, which will fully
enable 16 MW of Al grade capacity at DataVita’s existing Tier III data centre. This significant commitment underscores both the scale
of the opportunity and the confidence placed in DataVita’s platform. The designation highlights the national strategic importance of the
site and its capability to support large scale Al and high performance computing (HPC) infrastructure. CoreWeave Inc. has been
confirmed as DataVita’s strategic partner for the initial phase of the Al Growth Zone build out, supporting the expansion of specialist
compute capacity across the designated zone.

Aiter the balance sheet date, DataVita secured a £44.9 million partnership with Glasgow City Council to modemise and maintain the
city's digital infrastructure, In addition, the company entered into a strategic partnership with University College London (UCL) to hast
its new £19.5 million supercomputer, further reinforcing DataVita’s status as a leading provider of large scale, high performance
compute infrastructure.

The Board believes that Datavita is exceptionally well positioned to benefit from the Al Growth Zone designation and its widening
portfolio of strategic partnerships. This confidence is underpinned by DataVita’s scalable data centre platform, the complementary
renewable energy capabilities of the group's Renewables division, and continued strong demand for data centre and Al grade capacity
across the UK and Europe. The directors remain confident in the group's resilience, financial strength, and ability to navigate potential
macroeconomic headwinds.
