 The project The Ravenscraig Data Centre Campus will combine state-of-the-art data halls with an on-site Battery Energy Storage System (BESS). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Located on land to the east and west of the Ravenscraig masterplan, the development will connect directly to high-capacity substations at Wishaw and Newarthill, positioning it perfectly to meet Scotland’s growing demand for secure data capacity and clean energy storage. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 We are proposing a 550MW sustainably designed data campus alongside a 150MW battery energy storage system, designed to deliver critical digital services while supporting Scotland’s net zero and regeneration ambitions. 
 Purpose-built to the highest technical, environmental, and safety standards, t he campus will: 
 
 Store renewable energy and deliver it instantly when required (the BESS will be consented via the Scottish Government’s Energy Consents Unit (ECU) through a Section 36 application). 
 Ensure uninterrupted, ultra-low latency service. 
 Provide 24/7 operational resilience, even during fluctuations in the wider electricity network. 
 
 By integrating advanced digital capability with large-scale energy storage, the Ravenscraig Data Centre Campus will be a cornerstone of Scotland’s clean energy future and a catalyst for local regeneration. 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 The data centre illustrative site masterplan The Ravenscraig campus is split across two areas, east and west, each with its own masterplan. The eastern site is larger and accommodates six data halls, while the smaller western plots host three buildings integrated into the local road network.

 Why this site has been chosen Strategic location Ravenscraig offers a rare combination of space, infrastructure, and regeneration opportunity that makes it ideally suited for a development of national significance. Grid resilience The site has direct access to a high-capacity dual grid connection from the nearby Newarthill and Wishaw substations, providing the level of power resilience essential for data centre operations. Policy alignment The proposals support both national and local priorities, including Scotland’s Net Zero targets, ambitions for advanced digital infrastructure, and inclusive economic growth.
