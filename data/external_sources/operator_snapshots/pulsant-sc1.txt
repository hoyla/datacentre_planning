 Edinburgh South Gyle SC-1 
 The most connected data centre in Scotland 
 Edinburgh South Gyle SC–1 is Scotland’s most connected data centre, enabling clients with substantial connectivity requirements to seamlessly cross-connect to over 22 on-site carriers, including the LINX Scotland internet exchange and Megaport. This highly resilient site is located just 6.5 miles west of central Edinburgh, this facility is conveniently accessible by road and rail, and can be easily reached from Edinburgh Airport via tram. Flexible colocation options include private suites, high-density racks, full, half, and quarter racks and private cages. 
 SC–1 is part of Pulsant’s network of 14 interconnected UK data centres, connected via the Pulsant Edge Fabric, a 100Gb national network for low-latency access to 1,600+ cloud services, network provider and business partners. 
 Scotland is home to some of the fastest-growing technology and financial organisations in the UK, boasting a highly educated and skilled workforce in tech roles. Pulsant has the largest footprint of highly connected data centres in Scotland with three located on the outskirts of Edinburgh.
