 London 4 Data Centre

 Hyperscale data centre with 31MW under construction 
 
 
 
 Colt DCS is currently building the first phase of our London Hayes Campus with Colt London 4 Data Centre now under construction. 
 This site has been designed to a Colt tier 3 specification and upon completion, it will deliver 31MW of IT power across 5 floors. 
 Built to meet the city’s growing appetite for cloud technology and the recent surge in AI demand, our Colt London 4 Data Centre is an excellent facility for cloud and AI service providers who require scalable capacity to support high performance computing (HPC) and increasing IT workloads. With phase 1 expected to complete in 2025, the site is already fully let.

 Key features of our Colt London 4 Data Centre
 
 
 With this site already fully let, our customer will benefit from design flexibility and tailored solutions to meet their specific requirements. Some of the key features once this data centre is delivered will include: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Scale and Power 
 
 With data halls across 5 floors and 31MW of IT power, the site has been built to support our customer's strategic growth objectives offering scalable capacity.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Location 
 
 Constructed in Hayes, this facility is in ideal proximity to the city to offer our customer low latency to other data centres. International customers can also benefit from Hayes being approximately 15 minutes from London Heathrow Airport.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Connectivity 
 
 To support our customer's network requirements, this hyperscale data centre will have access to a high concentration of diverse fibre routes and the very best connectivity options.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Sustainability 
 
 Powered using renewable energy and various environmentally friendly processes and procedures to operate the infrastructure, this site will bring our customer closer to reducing their carbon footprint and meeting their sustainability goals. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Operational Excellence 
 
 Operated by experienced staff who possess first-class technical capabilities and a wealth of knowledge, our customers will have peace of mind knowing that their IT infrastructure is in capable hands.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Onboarding & Support 
 
 From consultation, to design and implementation, our team are on hand to support our customer throughout the entire process and beyond the handover of their new white space.
