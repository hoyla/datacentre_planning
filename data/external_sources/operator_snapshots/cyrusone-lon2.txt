 CyrusOne's LON2 data center, conveniently located west of London in Prologis Park Heathrow, offers customized data center solutions to clients. 
 
 
 Strategically located in Prologis Park Heathrow, our state-of-the-art purpose-built LON2 Data Center delivers 14 MW of IT capacity to 7,484 sq. m. of technical space within one building across two stories. Each data hall is meticulously engineered for maximum efficiency and scalability, tailored to meet unique customer requirements. 
 The facility achieves low PUE through a chilled water system with external adiabatic free cooling chillers and features advanced power and building monitoring systems. A team of on-site mechanical and electrical (M&E) engineers is available 24x7x365, implementing rigorous Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM) programs to guarantee peak performance.
