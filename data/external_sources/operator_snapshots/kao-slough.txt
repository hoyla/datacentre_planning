 Slough 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Ideally positioned in the UK’s data centre heartland, Kao Data’s KLON-06 facility on Slough Trading Estate, provides 20MW of Tier 3 equivalent colocation. This connectivity-rich, and sought-after location is sited within the West London Availability Zone, popular with Tier 1 and 2 hyperscale cloud providers. KLON-06 represents a sizeable and scalable option for continued enterprise or cloud deployments, with all capacity operating to an SLA-backed PUE of 1.25, and all IT-load powered by 100% renewable energy.
