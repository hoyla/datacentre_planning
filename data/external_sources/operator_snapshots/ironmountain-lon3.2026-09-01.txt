 Strategically positioned in the highly interconnected Slough data center corridor just west of the M25, Iron Mountain's LON-3 delivers 25 MW of AI-ready critical IT capacity across six GPU-ready data halls totaling 5,200 square meters (55,972 square feet). This three-story, highly secure facility will open later in 2026. Whether you're a hyperscale cloud provider, enterprise, or government agency, LON-3 provides the space, power, security, and compliance standards your organization demands. This carrier-neutral colocation facility offers connectivity to the LINX exchange and dark fiber access to hundreds of networks, clouds, and service providers.

 Download Spec Sheet Frequently asked questions What makes LON-3 unique compared to other London data centers? LON-3 combines 25 MW of AI-ready infrastructure with strategic positioning in the Slough data center corridor, offering exceptional connectivity through the LINX exchange and dark fiber access to 100+ providers. The facility features comprehensive compliance certifications, 100% renewable energy, and BREEAM green building standards, backed by Iron Mountain's 75 years of security expertise.
 What other data center facilities does Iron Mountain have in the United Kingdom? Iron Mountain operates multiple data centers across the UK, including LON-1 and LON-2 data centers on the same Slough Trading Estate campus as LON-3. Our UK data centres portfolio provides comprehensive colocation in London and throughout the United Kingdom. To explore our complete portfolio of UK data centres locations and colocation services, visit ironmountain.com/en-gb/data-centers .
 What is the total capacity and size of LON-3? LON-3 provides 25 megawatts of critical IT capacity across 5,200 square meters (55,972 square feet) of data center space. The 2.5-acre campus features a three-story building with six data halls, all designed to support both CPU and GPU workloads with AI-ready power and cooling infrastructure.
 What security and compliance measures are in place at LON-3? LON-3 maintains the industry's most comprehensive compliance program, including ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 22301, ISO 27001, ISO 50001, ISO 14064, SOC 1, SOC 2, HITRUST, PCI-DSS, and BREEAM certifications. Physical security includes 24/7/365 on-site staff, biometric access controls, CCTV surveillance, and multi-layer perimeter security, all backed by Iron Mountain's seven decades of experience protecting confidential data. When will LON-3 be available for customer deployments? LON-3 is scheduled for phased opening later in 2026, with data halls becoming available soon. This staged approach allows customers to plan their deployments strategically and scale infrastructure as their requirements grow.
