 Three Planned Data Centres at Colt DCS’ London Hayes Campus 
 
 Development is planned for three additional data centres on our London Hayes Campus with London 6, 7 and 8. This will take the total number of data centres on the site to five. 
 The planning and development process is well underway, with construction expected to start on site in 2026 and the first phase of delivery in 2028. Delivery of buildings can be altered to meet customer requirements. 
 Once complete, these data centres will add 98MW to the available IT power at our London Hayes Campus, taking the total capacity to 160MW. 
 
 
 Contact Us 
 
 
 
 


 
 
 
 Development Highlights 
 
 London 6 will deliver up to 9 x 1,000m2 data halls and 27MW of IT power. 
 London 7 will have 15 x 1,000m2 data halls and up to 48MW of IT Power. 
 London 8 will have 4 x 1,000m2 halls and 4 x 680m2 halls with up to 23MW of IT Power 
 Average power density can be designed to be 3-4.5 KW/sqm. 
 1.35 Annualised design PUE.
