 Northolt 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Kao Data’s Northolt facility enjoys a prime West London location within the M25 perimeter with excellent transport connections to the city and London Heathrow. KLON-05 provides 4MW of Tier 3 equivalent, concurrently maintainable colocation, all operated at a low PUE of 1.25 and powered by 100% renewable energy ensuring a low-carbon computing footprint. With demand for high performance colocation increasing within West London, our Northolt facility is now fully occupied.
