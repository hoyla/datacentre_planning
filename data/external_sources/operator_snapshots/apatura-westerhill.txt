 The project The Westerhill Data Centre Campus will combine a state-of-the-art hyperscale data centre campus with an on-site Battery Energy Storage System (BESS). 
 
 Located within the adopted Westerhill Regeneration Masterplan to the north-east of Bishopbriggs, the east of Cadder and the west of Lenzie, the site will connect to the Lambhill substation to the south-west, positioning it perfectly to meet Scotland’s growing demand for secure data capacity and clean energy storage. The BESS will be consented via the Scottish Government’s Energy Consents Unit (ECU) through a Section 36 application. 
 
 We are proposing a 300MW Data Centre Campus alongside a 500MW BESS, designed to deliver critical digital services while supporting Scotland’s Net Zero and regeneration ambitions. Purpose-built to the highest technical, environmental, and safety standards, the campus will: 
 
 Store renewable energy and deliver it instantly when required 
 Ensure uninterrupted, ultra-low latency service 
 Provide 24/7 operational resilience, even during fluctuations in the wider electricity network 
 
 By integrating advanced digital capability with large-scale energy storage, the Westerhill Data Centre Campus will be a cornerstone of Scotland’s clean energy future and a catalyst for local regeneration. 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 The data centre site masterplan The data centre site masterplan sets out our vision for how the facility will be designed, built, and integrated within Westerhill, balancing world-class digital infrastructure with community, environmental, and regeneration goals. 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 Why this site has been chosen Strategic location Located within the Westerhill Regeneration Area, the proposed AI data energy campus offers a strategic opportunity to bring masterplan enabled employment land into productive use. Grid resilience The site has direct access to a high-capacity grid connection from the nearby Lambhill substation. This provides the level of power essential for data centre operations. Policy alignment The proposals support both national and local priorities, including Scotland’s net zero targets, ambitions for advanced digital infrastructure, and inclusive economic growth.
