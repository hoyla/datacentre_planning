Delivering 78MW of IT load across four separate data centres, the VIRTUS 
Saunderton Campus is strategically located close to the existing Slough data 
centre ecosystem. Covering more than 50 acers of land, the campus sets a 
new benchmark in mega scale data centre capacity for cloud and AI in Europe. 
With a blank canvas, the facilities will utilise the latest in cooling technologies 
to cater for customers of all size.
