 Edinburgh SC-3 
 Colocation in Scotland's capital city 
 Edinburgh SC-3 is a highly resilient and accredited data centre, located 12 miles west of central Edinburgh. This site provides a lower cost alternative to SC-1 and SC-2 with 6 on-site carriers. This facility has excellent access via road and rail networks and can be easily reached from Edinburgh Airport. Flexible colocation options include full, half and quarter racks. 
 SC-3 is part of Pulsant’s network of 14 interconnected UK data centres, connected via the Pulsant Edge Fabric, a 100Gb national network for low-latency access to 1,600+ cloud services, network providers, and business partners. 
 Scotland is home to some of the fastest-growing technology and financial organisations in the UK, boasting a highly educated and skilled workforce in tech roles. Pulsant has the largest footprint of highly connected data centres in Scotland with three located on the outskirts of Edinburgh.
