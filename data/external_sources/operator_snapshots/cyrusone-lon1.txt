 CyrusOne's purpose-built data center in London offers customized solutions and technical sophistication. 
 
 
 Strategically located in Slough, UK, and close to Heathrow Airport, our LON1 data center delivers 9 MW of IT capacity to 4,309 sq. m. of world-class technical space within one building over two stories. Each data hall is meticulously engineered for maximum efficiency and scalability, tailored to meet unique customer requirements. 
 The facility offers ultra-low PUE through Indirect Adiabatic Air Cooling and features advanced power and building monitoring systems. A team of on-site mechanical and electrical (M&E) engineers is available 24x7x365, implementing rigorous Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM) programs to guarantee peak performance.
