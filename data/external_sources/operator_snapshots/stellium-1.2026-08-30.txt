 Stellium Data Centres 
 Stellium 1 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Covering an area of more than 4,264m2 and divided into four 4MW IT data halls whilst being fully scalable, it’s one of the most efficient colocation data centres in the UK. 
 Connected direct to the 275KV National Grid , Stellium Data Centres ensure low-cost, ultra-reliable power at whatever levels you need. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Stellium Data Centre 1 Facts: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 80 MWs (designed to scale to 180 MWs) of REGO certified renewable power 
 Tier-3+ designed 
 OCP Ready TM ISO- 27001 , ISO- 90001, ISO- 140001, ISO- 22301, ISO- 200001 Cyber Essentials+ and PSN Network certified facility 
 4,264 m2 of IT space (traditional water-cooled) 
 4 x 1066 m2 IT sectors 
 AquaComms North Atlantic Loop & Altibox NO-UK CLS (Cable Landing Stations, inside our highly secure data centre) 
 2 x Meet-Me rooms 
 PUE rating at 1.24 
 Support from low to high density racks ( including HPC ) 
 BREEAM Rated Excellent 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Why Newcastle? 
 
 
 
 
 
 There are many reasons to choose Newcastle as your location of choice . Why continue to locate in congested London with increasing power costs and repeated issues with infrastructure when: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Stellium provides 80 MWs (designed to scale to 180 MWs) of REGO certified renewable power at Stellium Newcastle. 
 Our location in the North East is one of the lowest carbon intensity regions in the United Kingdom. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Source : National Grid ESO 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Stellium has no cooling issues – unlike London data centres who have experienced major cooling issues, Stellium have had none. 
 Stellium’s Tier 3+ data centre in Newcastle operates in less hostile temperatures than London, rarely exceeding 22 deg C v London’s 32 deg C ( June 2023 ).The consequences are lower cooling bills and a cooling system that always works well within its design parameters. 
 Stellium provides metered power contracts – in London the infrastructure to give metered contracts is not always available. Energy is now 70% of your colocation cost so this is a major issue for your business in terms of transparency. 
 Stellium provides mobilisation time for contracts up to 1.3MW in 4 weeks. 
 One of the most accredited data centres in the UK, including OCP Ready™ certification for AI and HPC data centre services 
 Stellium has no latency congestion . In fact we provide some of the lowest latency now available in the UK. As the landing station for the latest international subsea fibre cables, Newcastle is now closer to New York than London is. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Power usage effectiveness (PUE): 
 Delivers a verified design PUE of 1.24 when operated within the industry-recommended temperature and humidity parameters. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Tier 3+ design: 
 Our colocation data centres meets all Tier 3+ requirements of concurrent maintainability, diverse engineering and avoidance of single points of failure. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Campus electrical power: 
 80MVA via four 20MVA 11kV feeds to two dedicated SSE switch-rooms, with scalability up to 180MVA. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 IT power density & Cooling: 
 11MW IT power delivering 8kW racks (3kW per square metre) across each data hall, with a local capability of up to 50kW per rack. 
 HPC cooling of client racks from 10 to 100kW using in-row/rear door chilled water cooling. 
 Bespoke designs available for fully immersive cooling up to 200kW per rack can be supported.
