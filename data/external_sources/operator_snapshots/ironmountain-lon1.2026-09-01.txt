 Download Spec Sheet Frequently asked questions What makes LON-1 unique among London area data centers? LON-1 was originally built to Fortune 1000 banking standards for Credit Suisse, providing infrastructure reliability and security that exceeds typical colocation facilities. The facility combines this banking-grade foundation with extensive upgrades, carrier-neutral connectivity to 15+ on-site providers, direct LINX exchange access, and a strategic location in the heart of the Slough data center corridor—one of Europe's most densely interconnected network hubs. This heritage makes LON-1 an ideal location for financial services organizations that require the highest levels of physical security, operational resilience, and regulatory compliance for their mission-critical infrastructure.
 What other data center facilities does Iron Mountain operate in the UK and Europe? Iron Mountain operates two additional london facilities (LON-2 and LON-3), along with data centers in Amsterdam, Frankfurt, and Madrid. This European footprint enables customers to deploy distributed architectures with consistent service levels, private network connectivity between facilities, and access to our full portfolio of colocation, connectivity, and cloud services. Visit our UK data centres page to explore all locations.
 How much power and space is available at LON-1? LON-1 is a 10,400 square meter (approximately 112,000 square feet) facility with four data halls and 8.75 MW of total power capacity. The facility provides flexible deployment options from individual colocation cabinets to dedicated cages on traditional raised floor infrastructure. Power delivery features N+1 block redundancy to ensure continuous operations.
 What security and compliance measures are in place at LON-1? LON-1 implements multi-layered physical security, including two-factor access (badge + PIN) to enter the facility and three-factor authentication (badge + PIN + biometrics) to access data halls through security vestibules. The facility maintains comprehensive compliance certifications, including SOC 2 Type II, SOC 3, PCI-DSS, HITRUST, and multiple ISO standards (27001, 22301, 50001, 14001, 45001). All security and compliance controls are regularly audited to ensure they meet the rigorous standards required by financial services, healthcare, and other regulated industries. How does LON-1 support sustainability and environmental goals? Iron Mountain Data Centers is committed to environmental responsibility and helping customers achieve their sustainability objectives. LON-1 holds ISO 14001 (Environmental Management) and ISO 50001 (Energy Management) certifications. Customers can access our Green Power Pass program to report renewable energy usage and carbon reductions based on Iron Mountain's renewable energy contracts. As a top 10 EPA Green Power Partner in tech/telecom and signatory to the Renewable Energy Buyers Principles, we're invested in sustainable operations across our global portfolio.
 What connectivity options are available at your London data centre (LON-1)? LON-1 offers extensive carrier-neutral connectivity, including 15 on-site carriers, direct connectivity to the LINX internet exchange, and dark fiber access to over 100 additional network service providers, cloud platforms, and IT service providers. The facility supports IP transit with redundant upstream providers, 10Gb and 100Gb metro wave services, campus cross-connects, and private network transport to other Iron Mountain data centers. With less than 1ms latency to the LD4 carrier hotel, LON-1 provides optimal access to cloud on-ramps from major providers including AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.
