 Rotherham YH-1 
 Colocation for Yorkshire and the surrounding area 
 Our Rotherham colocation facility is strategically located for connections into Yorkshire, Manchester and the Midlands. Easily accessible by road and rail, this data centre benefits from a recent data hall refit to offer best-in-class infrastructure for organisations looking for an alternative to hosting production or disaster recovery infrastructure in Leeds. 
 YH-1 is part of Pulsant’s network of 14 interconnected UK data centres, connected via the Pulsant Edge Fabric, a 100Gb national network for low-latency access to 1,600+ cloud services, network providers, and business partners. 
 The North of England is home to a number of thriving cities and businesses are looking to capitalise on an increasing talent pool combined with lower operational costs. Pulsant has a significant footprint of facilities in the North with 5 data centres spread across 4 major locations, offering low-latency edge connectivity to regional technology hubs.
