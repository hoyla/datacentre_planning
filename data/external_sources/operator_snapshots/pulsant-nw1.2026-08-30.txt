 Manchester NW-1 
 Colocation in the heart of Manchester 
 Our Manchester NW-1 facility, situated in the UK's second largest economic city, is a highly resilient and accredited data centre with access to over 9 on-site connectivity partners. The site offers direct access to LINX Manchester for ultra-fast interconnection to members of this growing digital ecosystem. 
 NW-1 is part of Pulsant's network of 14 interconnected UK data centres, connected via the Pulsant Edge Fabric, a 100Gb national network for low-latency access to 1,600+ cloud services, network providers, and business partners. 
 The North of England is home to a number of thriving cities and businesses are looking to capitalise on an increasing talent pool combined with lower operational costs. Pulsant has a significant footprint of facilities in the North with 5 data centres spread across 4 major locations, offering low-latency edge connectivity to regional technology hubs.
