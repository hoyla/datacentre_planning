Union Park Union Park is Ark’s high-performance, AI-ready data centre campus in Hayes, blending onsite solar, living roofs, and 100% renewable power. The facility delivers secure, low-carbon infrastructure to meet ever-growing digital needs. 0 MW Total compute capacity Updated: 1.1.25 0 Data centres Updated: 1.1.25 0 MT Carbon reduced Updated: 1.1.25 0 RFS Updated: 1.1.25 About Located at the Bulls Bridge Industrial Estate in Hayes, Union Park embodies Ark’s commitment to secure, sustainable data centres. This 56,000m² campus employs onsite solar, advanced air cooling, and rainwater harvesting, alongside living roofs and walls that enhance biodiversity. With 100% renewable power from the grid and HVO backup generators cutting emissions by up to 90%, Union Park meets rising digital demands while championing a low-impact footprint. Technology Engineered to achieve optimal efficiency and density, leveraging cutting-edge cooling technologies to maintain peak performance while minimising environmental impact.
