 Manchester 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Our new data centre, proposed for the Greater Manchester borough of Stockport, represents an investment of £350 million and when it becomes operational in Q3-2027, will be Northern England’s largest facility. Following our award-winning high-performance design, efficiency and sustainability ethos, the data centre will provide capacity for more than 4,000 racks, all powered by 100% renewable energy. Download our Why Manchester? whitepaper and find out why Kao Data chose the city for our most ambitious data centre project yet.

 Why Manchester? 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Scalability 
 
 Northern England’s largest data centre, offering multi-megawatt Technology Suites designed to hyperscale standards 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 AI & HPC Provision 
 
 Designed to NVIDIA DGX-Ready standards, KMAN-01 will offer capacity for GPU-accelerated AI and HPC. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 TCO Focused 
 
 Our basis of design provides ultra-efficient, customisable hosting environments operating at a PUE of 1.2. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Connectivity 
 
 As the second most connected city in the UK, KMAN-01 will access a variety of high-density, low latency fibre routes. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Sustainability 
 
 KMAN-01 will set a new benchmark for sustainability, utilising BREEAM ‘Excellent’ architecture and 100% renewable energy. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Media Specialism 
 
 Located close to Manchester’s media, broadcasting, SFX and CGI clusters, KMAN-01 is the ideal home.
