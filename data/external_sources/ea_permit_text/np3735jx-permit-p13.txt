IC3 The Operator shall carry out a review of the site’s secondary and 
tertiary containment systems which serves the oil and diesel storage 
tanks. The review shall compare the system’s design, method of 
construction and integrity against the standards outlined in CIRIA 
guidance C736 – Containment Systems for the Prevention of Pollution 
or an equivalent industry standard.   
A written report of the review shall be submitted to the Environment 
Agency for written approval which details: 
• the review’s findings and recommendations; 
• proposals for the implementation of recommended 
improvements; and 
• timescales for implementation of improvements. 
The operator shall implement the recommended improvements to the 
secondary and tertiary containment systems within the timescales 
approved by the Environment Agency.
