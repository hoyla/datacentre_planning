There is planning permission for the construction of residential apartments on Maylands Avenue, directly 
opposite the data centre. When completed, these will be the closest residential receptors to the data centre. 
Chiltern Beechwoods (Special Area of Conservation) is located approximately 8km north-west from the data 
centre. There are a number of Local Wildlife Sites, Local Nature Reserves and Ancient Woodlands within 
2 km of the data centre.
