There are oil/ water interceptors at the site. The main interceptor covering the area of the tank farm, loading 
bay and surrounding hardstanding is a Class 1 full retention interceptor with an oil capacity of 10,000 litres.  
Areas of hard surfacing away from the tank farm and loading bay are discharged through a separate surface 
water system which discharges through Class 1 bypass interceptors with an oil capacity of 60 litres.  
Uncontaminated surface water from the site, drains to soakaways and to the surface water sewer along Ajax 
Avenue, which ultimately discharges into the Jubilee River, a tributary of the River Thames. The site is 
covered in hardstanding to reduce the likelihood of potential leaks contaminating the underlying soils.  
The installation is located in Slough, National Grid Reference SU 95949 80764. The surrounding area is a 
mix of industrial and commercial uses. However, there are residential properties, gyms, schools and day 
nurseries nearby. There are also 4 protected European sites within 10km, 4 local sites within 2km and 1 
protected species within 500m. The data centre is not situated in an Air Quality Management Area (AQMA), 
but there are AQMAs within 2km of the site. The closest AQMA is Slough AQMA No. 3 . This is located 347m 
south east of the data centre.
