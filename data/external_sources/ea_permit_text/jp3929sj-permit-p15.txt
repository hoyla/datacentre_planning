IC7 Site Drainage 
 
The operator shall submit a written report to the Environment Agency 
for confirmation. 
 
The report must contain: 
• Evidence that a CCTV pipeline assessment has been carried 
out on all sewer pipelines within the installation boundary, 
mapping all drains, lines and reporting on their condition. 
• Confirmation that the surface drainage from all areas housing 
generators and fuel storage tanks, passes through the 
interceptor before being emitted via emission points W1 and 
W2. 
 
Submit the written report 
within 8 months of issue 
of EPR/JP3929SJ/A001 
or as agreed in writing 
with the Environment 
Agency
