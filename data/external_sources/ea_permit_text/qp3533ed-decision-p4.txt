Use of Diesel Generators  
 
Santander compared seven options for the technology to supply an 
independent emergency electricity supply to Carlton Park Data Centre 1 and 
Data Centre 2 as detailed below:  
 
• Option 1 – Diesel engine generators run on fuel oil 
• Option 2 – LPG spark ignition generators 
• Option 3 – Piped natural gas spark ignition generators
