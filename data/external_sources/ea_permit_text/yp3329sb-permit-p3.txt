The below ground bulk fuel storage tanks are replenished from deliveries of gas oil/ HVO from tankers to the 
site. The tanker unloading bay is an impermeable concrete hardstanding area bound by slot drainage 
leading to a below ground 45 litre capacity bypass interceptor with alarm in case of spillages or loss of 
containment from the road tanker, this then connects into the site foul drainage system.  
There are three alarmed oil/ water interceptors on site, serving the generator locations and the fuel delivery 
point. There are two 10,000 litre full retention interceptors serving the LHR11 generator building and LHR12 
generator gantry area drainage, and a 45 litre capacity bypass interceptor serving the fuel offloading area. 
Uncontaminated surface water from the site, drains to a soakaway, the foul public sewer and the surface 
water public sewer before combining in Victoria Road into one combined foul and surface water public 
sewer. The combined sewer discharges to Beckton Wastewater Treatment Works and ultimately the River 
Thames. The site is covered in hardstanding to reduce the likelihood of potential leaks contaminating the 
underlying soils.  
The Installation is located within a predominately mixed industrial and commercial area, known as Park 
Royal, within the London Borough of Ealing at national grid reference TQ 21147 82397. 
There are also 2 protected European sites within 10km, 31 local sites within 2km and 1 protected species 
within 500m. The data centre is situated in an Air Quality Management Area (AQMA) (Ealing AQMA) 
declared by the London Borough of Ealing in 2000 for annual mean NO2 and 24-hour PM10.
