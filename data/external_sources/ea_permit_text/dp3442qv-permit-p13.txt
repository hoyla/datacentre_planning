Table S1.4 Pre-operational measures 
Reference Pre-operational measure 
PO1 At least 1 month before operation the operator shall submit a commissioning plan to

within the site’s permitted regular testing regime, the operator shall submit an 
environmental risk assessment for approval by the Environment Agency, 
demonstrating that the environmental risks during commissioning are minimised and 
remain not significant. The commissioning of the engines shall not begin pri or to 
receiving written approval to the plan and associated environmental risk assessment 
by the Environment Agency.
