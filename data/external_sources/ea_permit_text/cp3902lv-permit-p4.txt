All SBGs (except the 12 x 4.1 MWth LON1A phase 1 SBGs), are fitted with SCR NOx abatement systems. 
The 16 LON1A SBGs SCR system uses IMS-eco technology, which has a target of reducing NOx emissions 
by 79%. Each SCR abatement system will take between 5 and 10 minutes to reach the 79% NO x reduction, 
based on a load of between 75% and 100%. The SCR NOx abatement system will reduce the NOx emissions 
to 667mg/m3 per generator at 100% load and 5% oxygen (257mg/m3 at 15% oxygen).   
Each SCR abatement system will have a 2,000 litre AdBlue bulk tank, located beneath the inlet attenuator of 
the SBG container unit.  
To prevent ammonia slip, Ammonia Slip Catalysts (ASC) are used. The ASC technology is designed to 
control up to 20 ppm of ammonia in the exhaust gas from SCR, based on the SCR design target which is 
79% NOx reduction. 
The 24 LON1B SBGs will be supplied with SCR abatement, designed to reduce NOx emissions to meet the 
Medium Combustion Plant Directive (MCPD) emission limit for NOx of 190 mg/m3 @ 15% oxygen. At the 
time of variation EPR/CP3902LV/V003, the SCR abatement system had not been selected and a pre-
operational condition was set to address this.
