Table S1.4 Pre-operational measures 
Reference Pre-operational measures 
PO2 Containment 
At least one month before operation the operator shall undertake a review of all liquid 
storage, bunds and secondary containment at the site and submit a written report to 
the Environment Agency for assessment and written approval.  
The review shall be carried out by a qualified structural engineer and compare all liquid 
storage, bunds and secondary containment against the standards set out in CIRIA 
guidance: Containment systems for the prevention of pollution (C736).
