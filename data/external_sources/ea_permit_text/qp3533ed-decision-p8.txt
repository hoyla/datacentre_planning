Screening out emissions which are insignificant 
 
In accordance with Environment Agency guidance H1 Annex F, the following 
emissions are considered insignificant as the short term impact is < 10% of 
the relevant short term EQS’s/EAL’s and < 1% of the long term EQS/EAL. 
These are:   
• SO
2 short term (15 min, 1hr and 24 hr); 
• PM10 short term; and 
• PM10 long term.
