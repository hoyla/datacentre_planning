# What we know about Dartford

*Reporter brief, 26 August 2026. Every figure below is quoted from a
document in the corpus; the document reference follows each claim. As of
25 August 2026 this scheme had been reported nowhere.*

## The scheme

On **1 July 2026** the Secretary of State issued a **Section 35
direction** for a data centre campus on land adjacent to **New Barn
Road, Dartford, Kent**. The effect of a direction is that the scheme is
treated as a Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project: it will be
consented through a Development Consent Order decided by the Secretary
of State, not by Dartford Borough Council. The applicant is **CSE52
Limited**, which the supporting statement states is *"a wholly owned
subsidiary of Clearstone Energy Limited"* (Supporting Statement for
S35, p3). The direction was requested by Burges Salmon LLP on the
company's behalf by emails of 1 May and 3 June 2026.

The campus is described as *"an AI data centre campus of up to four data
centre buildings with a total power load of up to 300MW and IT load of
up to 240MW"* (Direction Granted, p1; repeated in the Supporting
Statement, p3, and the Response to Additional Information, p2). For
scale, the same statement argues that a facility of this size *"would be
a nationally significant"* proposition, and positions it as up to 5% of
projected national demand across approximately 180,000m² of floorspace
(p14, p18).

## Why the grid connection is the story

The scheme is grid-led rather than gas-led, which distinguishes it from
the other Section 35 campuses in the corpus. Three statements matter:

> "The Development benefits from a transitional connection offer from
> National Grid Electricity Transmission (NGET) for 300MW and will be
> allocated a firm, **Gate 2** connection offer by the National Energy
> System Operator (NESO) later this year."
> — Supporting Statement for S35, p8

> "The Site is situated approximately 500 metres south of the Northfleet
> East 400kV substation, which forms part of the National Grid
> Electricity Transmission network."
> — p7

> "one of the first data centres to connect directly to the UK's high
> voltage (400kV) electricity transmission network"
> — p23

A firm Gate 2 allocation is the scarce thing here. Under NESO's reformed
connections process, a Gate 2 offer means a project has cleared the
queue reform and holds a dated, firm connection — while other schemes
hold "transitional" offers with no such certainty. The claim that this
would be among the first data centres connecting *directly* to the
400kV transmission network, rather than through a distribution network
operator, is checkable and worth putting to NESO and NGET.

## Lines worth pursuing

**Has the Gate 2 offer been made?** The statement says it will be
allocated "later this year", written mid-2026. Whether NESO has since
confirmed it is a direct question to NESO, and the answer either way is
a story: a confirmed 300MW firm allocation to a single data centre, or a
scheme whose planning case rests on a connection it has not yet been
given.

**Who is Clearstone, and who is above it?** The supporting statement
credits Clearstone with having *"developed 2GW of low carbon energy
projects in the United Kingdom to date"* and *"2.5GW of UK data centre,
solar and battery storage projects in active development"*. Those are
the company's own figures, unverified by us. CSE52's own ownership chain
has not yet been pulled from Companies House — the SPV register built on
26 August covers 111 companies but not this one, and it is a single
query to add.

**The local dimension.** A Section 35 direction removes the decision
from Dartford Borough Council. Local consultees, the council itself, and
residents on New Barn Road may not know the scheme's consenting route
has changed. Worth asking the council what representations it made.

## What we hold, and what we do not

The corpus holds **six documents** for this scheme, all obtained from
the gov.uk publication rather than a planning portal, because no
planning application exists yet — the direction precedes the DCO:

| document | what it is |
|---|---|
| S35 Decision Letter, New Barn Road | the Secretary of State's decision |
| Direction Granted | the direction itself |
| Supporting Statement for S35 | the applicant's case — the source of most figures above |
| Request for Additional Information | MHCLG's questions |
| Response to Additional Information Request | the applicant's answers |
| Appendix 1: Ebbsfleet DCC Section 35 Plan | the site plan |

Source publication:

In the reader, the site is
`SITE-data-centre-campus-new-barn-road-dartford-section-35-direction-planning-act-2008`.
**Note it will not be findable by searching "Ebbsfleet"** — the site's
display name is generated from the gov.uk publication title, a known
defect. Search "Dartford" or "New Barn Road".

**What we do not have:** no DCO application (none has been filed), no
environmental statement, no water or cooling figures, no on-site
generation figures, and nothing from Dartford Borough Council. The 300MW
and 240MW figures are the applicant's own, stated in support of its
request, and have not been tested by an examining authority. There is no
independent confirmation of the NGET or NESO position in the corpus —
only the applicant's account of it.
