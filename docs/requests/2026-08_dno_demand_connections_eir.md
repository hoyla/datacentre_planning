# EIR — DNOs, demand connections ≥1 MW (one template, fourteen licensees)

**To:** each *licensed* distribution network operator plc — the
licensee, never the group management company, because the licensee is
the public authority carrying out functions of public administration
(supply of electricity network services under statutory licence) and
the body the EIR reaches.

**Framing:** Environmental Information Regulations 2004. Cite reg 5(6)
pre-emptively against section 105 of the Utilities Act 2000, exactly as
with NESO: near-absolute under FOIA, disapplied for environmental
information.
**Context:** the Embedded Capacity Registers all six groups publish
exclude demand connections (storage import aside), so data-centre load
is invisible in every open register. UKPN alone publishes gated
data-centre datasets (behind a portal login); no other group publishes
demand connections at all.

Dear [LICENSEE] information rights team,

Under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004, please provide
information about demand connections on your licensed distribution
network:

1. For each accepted, offered or connected demand connection of 1 MW
   and above (import capacity) since 1 January 2019: the connection
   site (address or substation and postcode area), the contracted or
   offered import capacity (MVA or MW), the connection status
   (connected, accepted-not-yet-connected, offered), the date of offer
   or acceptance, and the energisation date, actual or estimated.
2. The customer classification or use type your records hold for each
   (e.g. data centre), where recorded.
3. Where an equivalent extract already exists — for example a "large
   demand list" or data-centre dataset prepared for other purposes —
   that extract, as held.

You publish an Embedded Capacity Register for generation but demand
connections are excluded from it, so the information requested is not
otherwise available. It is environmental information under reg 2(1)(b):
electricity demand and the network reinforcement it drives are factors
affecting the elements of the environment.

If section 105 of the Utilities Act 2000 is considered, reg 5(6) EIR
disapplies it for environmental information. If reg 12(5)(e) is
considered, please balance per field with the reg 12(2) presumption in
favour of disclosure, and note that capacity headroom on a licensed
monopoly network is a matter of public administration rather than
commercial confidence; supply the remainder under reg 12(11).

Please supply the data as a spreadsheet (CSV or XLSX).

Yours faithfully,
[SENDER]
