# EIR — NESO, project-level demand connection queue

**To:** National Energy System Operator (NESO), information rights.
**Framing:** Environmental Information Regulations 2004. NESO is
publicly owned (since 2024) and carries out functions of public
administration relating to the environment; connection-queue
information bears directly on energy infrastructure and emissions, so
it is environmental information under reg 2(1).
**Why EIR and not FOIA:** section 105 of the Utilities Act 2000
restricts disclosure of information about a particular business and is
near-absolute under FOIA — but reg 5(6) of the EIR disapplies any such
statutory restriction for environmental information. Cite it
pre-emptively; it is the whole reason this request works.
**Context:** Ofgem's *Curate* consultation (July 2026) states ≈73 GW /
≈315 data-centre projects in the demand connection queue, but no
project-level demand register is published: TEC covers generation
only, and the DNOs' Embedded Capacity Registers exclude demand.
**Status:** DRAFT — not sent. Sender details are Luke's.

---

Dear NESO information rights team,

Under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004, please provide
project-level information from the demand connection queue for Great
Britain, covering transmission-connected demand and
distribution-connected demand known to NESO through the connections
reform process:

1. For each demand connection project in the queue at the most recent
   practicable date: project name, connection applicant, connection
   site or substation, region, contracted or applied-for capacity (MW
   or MVA), current queue status (including Gate 1/Gate 2 status under
   the reformed process), agreement type, and the offered or agreed
   connection date.
2. The technology or use classification recorded for each project
   (e.g. data centre, battery storage), and the date of any
   reclassification, including projects reclassified from storage to
   data centre.
3. The equivalent snapshot at May 2024, so the growth Ofgem describes
   in the Curate consultation (41 GW to 125 GW) can be examined at
   project level.

Ofgem's Curate consultation quantifies data centres at approximately
73 GW across approximately 315 projects in this queue. That aggregate
is published; this request asks for the register beneath it, which no
public source carries — NESO's TEC register covers generation only.

If NESO considers section 105 of the Utilities Act 2000 relevant,
please note reg 5(6) EIR: an enactment that would otherwise prohibit
disclosure "shall not apply" to environmental information. If reg
12(5)(e) is considered for any field, please apply the presumption in
favour of disclosure (reg 12(2)), balance each field separately, and
supply the remainder under reg 12(11) — a project's existence,
location, capacity and queue position describe demand on a public
network, not commercial strategy.

Please supply the data as a spreadsheet (CSV or XLSX).
