# FoI/EIR — site-level CCA consumption, data centres sector

**To:** Environment Agency (scheme administrator data holder), copied to
DESNZ (policy owner). Send to the EA first: it holds the target-unit
data; DESNZ holds scheme-level policy records.
**Framing:** Environmental Information Regulations 2004, and the
Freedom of Information Act 2000 insofar as any requested information is
not environmental. Electricity consumption data is environmental
information under EIR reg 2(1)(b) (a factor affecting the elements).
**Status:** DRAFT — not sent. Sender details and signature are Luke's.

---

Dear Environment Agency information rights team,

Under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 (and, insofar as
any of it is not environmental information, the Freedom of Information
Act 2000), please provide the following about the Climate Change
Agreements scheme's data centres sector (umbrella agreement held by
techUK):

1. For each target unit in the data centres sector, for each target
   period from 2021 to the most recent reported: the facility name or
   target-unit identifier, operator, site address or location, and the
   reported electricity consumption (kWh or TWh) underlying the
   published sector aggregate.
2. The list of current and former target units in the sector, with
   dates of entry and any termination.
3. Any guidance or correspondence defining what consumption the sector's
   target units report (site total, IT load, or another boundary).

techUK has publicly stated that this scheme makes the UK perhaps the
only country whose data-centre sector energy consumption is measured
using auditable data. The aggregate is published; this request asks for
the site-level series beneath it.

If you consider any of this exempt under EIR reg 12(5)(e) (commercial
confidentiality), please conduct the public-interest balance in the
knowledge that: the information is metered historical consumption, not
forward-looking commercial strategy; the facilities benefit from a
Climate Change Levy discount funded by other billpayers, which is a
public subsidy whose basis should be publicly auditable; and reg 12(9)
prevents reliance on 12(5)(e) for information on emissions, to which
metered electricity consumption directly relates. Where a specific
figure is genuinely exempt, please supply the remainder — reg 12(11) —
rather than withholding by class.

Please supply the data as a spreadsheet (CSV or XLSX) rather than PDF.

Yours faithfully,
